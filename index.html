A ROADMAP, NOT MORE GUESSWORK

Feel strong, clear and more like yourself.

The exhaustion that sleep doesn't fix. The brain fog, mood shifts and workouts that suddenly feel different. Perimenopause can bring a new set of rules. This programme helps you understand the changes and build supportive habits, one week at a time.

If you're tired of…

  • Being dismissed when you ask questions about your symptoms.
  • Following fitness and food advice that no longer feels right.
  • Waking up tired and feeling like your energy is unpredictable.
  • Trying random supplements without a clear plan.
  • Feeling like you're navigating this stage on your own.

And ready to…

  • Understand hormonal changes and supportive lifestyle habits.
  • Build strength with a realistic, progressive approach.
  • Create a calmer, more consistent sleep routine.
  • Make informed choices about food, recovery and supplements.
  • Have a sustainable plan — and a community beside you.

Small steps. Meaningful change.

08

Maintenance & next steps

Your sustainable habits, bounce-back plan and informed conversations about HRT.

WHY THIS APPROACH

Work with your body, not against it.

Perimenopause is a transition, not a personal failure. A one-size-fits-all plan can leave you feeling frustrated. The Power Reset brings together movement, nutrition, sleep, stress and practical education in one guided roadmap — so you can make thoughtful choices for the body and life you have today.

The programme is educational and is not a substitute for individual medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Individual experiences and results vary. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional about symptoms, supplements and HRT decisions.
